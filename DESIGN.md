# Design System

## Direction

The site uses a light scout-dossier visual language: the clarity of an official lineup card combined with the pacing of a concise game-day media guide. The page is factual, athletic, and immediately scannable without looking like a generic sports template.

## Audience and Job

The primary viewer is a university coach or scout. The first viewport must establish who Zalan is, his positions, recruiting year, physical profile, current teams, and the next action. Official statistics and source links carry the proof.

## Color

- Paper: `#F6F8FA` for the general canvas.
- White: `#FFFFFF` for clean evidence surfaces.
- Ink: `#102331` for primary text.
- Dugout navy: `#17364B` for the stat ribbon and major evidence fields.
- Federation blue: `#1769AA` for links, focus, and verified-source cues.
- Sky: `#DCECF7` for quiet feature fields.
- Rule: `#CFD9E0` for structure.
- Verified green: `#2E7558` only for availability/verified status.

## Typography

Geist Sans is the primary reading and display face. Geist Mono is reserved for measurements, statistics, season years, and the jersey mark. Headings use strong negative tracking and sentence case; body copy remains compact and readable.

## Layout

- Desktop sections use broad horizontal fields and a seven-percent page gutter.
- Section introductions use a narrow contextual rail and a dominant heading column.
- The hero is split between the recruiting case and a three-position contact sheet.
- Statistics use ruled tables and bands rather than generic cards.
- Mobile collapses the evidence into one clear reading order while retaining the stat ribbon and all links.

## Signature Element

The hero’s three-frame position strip—batting, catching, and first base—is the site’s memorable device. Until real images are supplied, honest labeled placeholders retain the final composition.

## Components and States

- Primary actions use solid dugout navy; secondary actions use white with a navy rule.
- Official external links are visibly underlined and use federation blue.
- Keyboard focus uses a clear blue ring.
- Motion is limited to small interaction feedback, and reduced-motion preferences are respected.
- Text selection is themed to the palette.

## Content Rules

Do not fabricate imagery, video, measurements, contact details, or recruiting documents. Keep verified competition data tied to its official source. Preserve the direct, coach-focused voice and avoid hype.
